Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions from homeowners and business founders partnering with A & A Design Studio in Ahmedabad.

How does turnkey interior design differ from contracting individual workers?

Turnkey design provides single-point accountability. Rather than managing separate carpenters, electricians, painters, and marble polishers, A & A Design Studio handles architectural design, procurement, quality control, timeline management, and site supervision with one guaranteed price and handover date.

What is the typical timeline for an apartment or villa interior?

A full 3BHK or 4BHK apartment renovation typically spans 60 to 90 working days from design sign-off. Larger 5,000+ sq.ft. private villas or corporate office suites take approximately 90 to 150 working days depending on civil modifications and custom millwork.

Do you take on projects for NRI clients living outside India?

Yes. A significant portion of our portfolio includes homes for non-resident Indian families. We conduct digital video walkthroughs, provide weekly photographic site progress logs, and coordinate all material choices digitally through high-resolution samples.
