Technical & Creative Mastery

Architectural Intelligence in Every Detail.

We don't merely decorate surfaces. We engineer spatial harmony through micro-climate analysis, high-fidelity lighting calculation, precision joinery, and turnkey project coordination.

Bioclimatic Space Planning

Architectural Core

Optimizing internal floor layouts for Ahmedabad's prevailing southwest wind currents, natural cross-ventilation, and privacy gradations. We eliminate awkward dead corridors and maximize unbroken sightlines.

Circulation Flow Vastu Alignment Daylight Ingress

3D Sun Simulation & Photorealism

Digital Twin Tech

Simulating the exact sun angle and shadow patterns for every month of the year in Ahmedabad. Using Corona Renderer and 3ds Max to calibrate window tinting, louver angles, and warm nighttime illumination before construction starts.

Millimeter Accuracy Raytraced Lighting 360° VR Tours

Direct Quarry Sourcing & Stone Engineering

Artisanal Mastery

Direct selection of natural stone blocks (Italian Travertine, Statuario, Makrana, Kota stone) and bookmatched exotic veneers. We calculate stone dry-lay patterns and anti-stain impregnating sealers for lasting permanence.

Bookmatching Honed Finishes Zero Synthetic Resins

Architectural Lighting & Lux Calculation

Atmospheric Science

Avoiding glaring central ceiling fixtures. We engineer three distinct layers: architectural coves (ambient), narrow-beam anti-glare downlights (accent), and decorative low-level fixtures (intimate task lighting with 95+ CRI).

CRI 95+ Color Accuracy Circadian 2700K Dimmers Concealed Aluminum Coves

Custom Millwork & Ergonomic Joinery

In-House Carpentry

Shop drawings drafted down to the millimeter for custom walk-in closets, dining tables, vanities, and TV wall consoles. Incorporating German concealed soft-close hardware with tested 100,000-cycle durability.

MEP Engineering & Turnkey Oversight

Civil Precision
