Modern Urban Residence

A 5,400 sq.ft. private villa in Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, sculpted around natural daylight, bespoke wood joinery, and an acoustic sanctuary design.

Location Bodakdev, Ahmedabad
Built-up Area 5,400 Sq. Ft. (4BHK)
Year Completed 2025
Scope Full Turnkey Interior
Lead Architect Mr. Nikunj Patel
Design Concept

An Ode to Warm Light & Understated Form

The homeowners, a multi-generational entrepreneurial family based in Ahmedabad, desired a dwelling that felt monumental yet warm—a sanctuary offering escape from city rhythms without sacrificing entertainment capabilities.

Our architectural approach began with spatial decompression. We opened compartmentalized walls to create continuous sightlines through the living pavilion, dining salon, and private courtyard. Every line was drawn with intention, concealing HVAC ducts within acoustic coves to maintain clean, unbroken ceilings.

The Spatial Challenge

The original civil structure featured low structural tie-beams and a dark central circulation corridor that fragmented the ground floor, causing harsh glare in western rooms and gloomy corners in northern wings.

The Architectural Solution

We incorporated soft radiused coves that gracefully float below the beams, integrating indirect 2700K LED wash lighting. Custom fluted glass screens and an open atrium skylight channeled natural illumination deep into the foyer without heat gain.
