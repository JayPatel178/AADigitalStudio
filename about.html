Two Decades of Craftsmanship

Architecture & Interiors Crafted with Integrity.

Founded in Ahmedabad in 2004, A & A Design Studio has spent over twenty years refining private residences and corporate environments across Gujarat.

Our Philosophy

Quiet Luxury, Climate Harmony, and Timeless Materials.

We believe a home should never feel like a transient showroom. True architectural luxury is experienced through proportion, the way Ahmedabad's golden afternoon sun filters through fluted screens, the acoustic calm of well-damped rooms, and the honest touch of natural stone and seasoned wood.

Under the leadership of Mr. Nikunj Patel, our studio fuses classical principles of spatial geometry and Vastu awareness with clean, contemporary detailing. Every custom millwork piece, cove detail, and fabric texture is selected to age gracefully with the family who inhabits it.

Milestones

Our 20-Year Evolution

Key moments in our studio's growth from boutique atelier to one of Ahmedabad's respected turnkey architectural practices.

2004

Studio Founded

Founded in Ahmedabad focusing on bespoke residential renovations and spatial planning for private residences.

2012

Commercial Expansion

Expanded into corporate office headquarters, luxury retail showrooms, and institutional projects across Gujarat.

2019

Swarnim Hub Atelier

Established our dedicated design atelier at Swarnim Business Hub-1 on Tragad Road, Gota, housing specialized 3D and sample libraries.

2026+

Bioclimatic Luxury

Pioneering low-carbon, bioclimatic residential interiors combining solar orientation, smart lighting, and indigenous materials.
