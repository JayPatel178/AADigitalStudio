Structured Workflow

How We Work: Predictable Excellence.

Building or renovating a luxury home should never feel chaotic. Our time-tested 6-step roadmap guarantees clarity, transparency, and exquisite quality at every milestone.

01

Initial Consultation & Spatial Audit

Week 1 • Discovery

We sit down together at our Gota studio or your property. We discuss your daily rituals, storage requirements, preferred materials, family routines, timeline constraints, and investment framework.

Key Deliverables & Outcomes:
  • • Comprehensive Client Lifestyle & Spatial Brief
  • • On-site laser dimensional survey and structural audit
  • • High-level scope, budget feasibility estimate & preliminary timeline
02

Concept & Spatial Planning

Weeks 2 – 3 • Geometry

Translating your brief into space. We test multiple 2D furniture layouts, study circulation paths, analyze natural light ingress, and establish harmonious zoning between public entertainment and private quarters.

Key Deliverables & Outcomes:
  • • 2 to 3 optimized 2D layout options with dimensional clearances
  • • Thematic concept moodboards (colors, textures, tactile references)
  • • Vastu alignment recommendations and natural ventilation analysis
03

Design Development & 3D Visualization

Weeks 4 – 6 • Visualisation

You see your home before a single nail is struck. We render full photorealistic 3D views of every room under morning sun, afternoon warmth, and evening mood lighting, complete with exact millwork and materials.

Key Deliverables & Outcomes:
  • • Photorealistic 3D renders for living, dining, kitchen, and suites
  • • Complete working architectural CAD drawings (electrical, plumbing, false ceiling)
  • • Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with transparent material pricing
04

Material & Furniture Selection

Weeks 6 – 7 • Curation

Our lead designers guide you through Ahmedabad's finest stone yards and fabric galleries. We dry-lay marble slabs together, touch natural veneers, and test hardware to confirm every tactile choice.

Key Deliverables & Outcomes:
  • • Physical sample board sign-off (stone, wood finish, brass, fabric)
  • • Sanitaryware, CP fittings & appliance specifications
  • • Finalized procurement schedule with vendor warranties
05

Precision On-Site Execution

Weeks 8 – 16 • Craftsmanship

Our full-time site supervisors and trusted craftspeople take over. Civil demolition, MEP routing, carpentry joinery, stone laying, and painting proceed in synchronized phases with zero guesswork.

Key Deliverables & Outcomes:
  • • Weekly video site walkthroughs for local or NRI homeowners
  • • Multi-point quality control audits before closing false ceilings
  • • Strict milestone tracking adhering to promised handover dates
06

Final Styling & White-Glove Handover

Week 17 • The Reveal

The transformation concludes. We conduct industrial deep cleaning, calibrate all lighting dimmers, hang artworks, drape curtains, and present you with your complete, turnkey sanctuary.

Key Deliverables & Outcomes:
  • • Comprehensive Handover Dossier (warranties, paint codes, electrical schematics)
  • • Snag-free white-glove walk-through inspection
  • • 1-Year Post-Handover Craftsmanship Guarantee
