Cookie & Storage Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • A & A Design Studio, Ahmedabad

1. Minimal Storage Philosophy

A & A Design Studio believes in clean, lightweight web experiences. We do not utilize third-party advertising trackers or invasive cross-site profiling cookies.

2. Functional Local Storage

We utilize the browser's native localStorage API solely to store your visual theme preference (aa_studio_theme: 'dark' | 'light'). This ensures that when you navigate between portfolio pages or revisit the site, your selected light or dark mode is immediately respected without a jarring screen flash.

3. Embedded Services

Pages displaying our Google Maps studio location embed standard Google Map elements, which may set technical session cookies required to render interactive map tiles and direction wayfinding.

4. Managing Browser Preferences

You may clear or disable local storage and cookies at any time through your browser's security settings. Doing so will simply reset the website to match your operating system's default visual appearance.
