Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • A & A Design Studio, Ahmedabad

1. Information We Collect

At A & A Design Studio ("we", "our", or "the Studio"), we collect personal details provided directly by you when you request an interior design consultation, including your name, email address, telephone number, property location in Ahmedabad or surrounding areas, and estimated budget parameters.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to communicate regarding your architectural or interior inquiry, prepare customized 2D/3D proposals, coordinate site measurement visits, and fulfill design contracts. We do not sell, lease, or monetize client data under any circumstances.

3. Client Privacy & Residential Discretion

We respect the confidentiality of private residences. Photography or project case studies of residential interiors are shared publicly only after explicit written consent from the homeowner, and any sensitive personal identifiers or exact street addresses are strictly obfuscated.

4. Local Storage & Cookies

This website uses client-side localStorage solely to remember your preferred visual theme (Dark or Light mode). No invasive tracking cookies are used to profile your web behavior.

5. Contacting Our Data Custodian

If you have any questions regarding your data or wish to have your consultation inquiry removed from our internal records, please contact us at info@aadesignstudio.in or write to our studio: A-325, Swarnim Business Hub-1, Tragad Road, Gota, Ahmedabad - 382470, Gujarat.
