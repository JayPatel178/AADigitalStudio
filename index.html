About Our Studio

Refined Architecture & Interiors Grounded in Purpose.

Established in August 2004, A & A Design Studio is an architectural and interior design atelier located in the thriving Gota area of Ahmedabad, opposite Global International School on Tragad Road.

Over the past two decades, our practice has grown around a central conviction: interior architecture should transcend fleeting decoration. We orchestrate proportions, bespoke materiality, bioclimatic ventilation, and precision lighting to create sanctuaries that elevate daily living.

How We Work

Our Proven 6-Step Turnkey Journey

A structured, collaborative framework designed to eliminate guesswork, budget creep, and stress.

01

Consultation

In-depth discovery session to understand your lifestyle, aesthetic aspirations, family requirements, and budget parameters.

02

Concept & Planning

Zoning diagrams, 2D architectural layouts, moodboards, and material palettes aligning form with function.

03

Design Development

High-fidelity 3D visualization, detailed electrical plans, plumbing schematics, and comprehensive working drawings.

04

Material Selection

Guided showroom walkthroughs to curate marbles, fluted glass, custom hardware, fabrics, and designer fixtures.

05

Execution & Civil

Full-time site supervision by our senior engineers ensuring millwork accuracy, structural integrity, and timeline tracking.

06

Final Reveal

Deep cleaning, art installation, custom accessory styling, and a celebratory white-glove handover of your bespoke sanctuary.
